Features

Everything to sort your invoices, nothing you don't need.

From the photo of a receipt to the export for your accountant, Tickami covers the whole life of an invoice. Every feature is included in the Free plan.

CaptureReadOrganiseTrackBank statementsExportProtect

Capture

Scan on the spot, wherever you are.

Edge detection

The iPhone scanner finds the document, crops it and straightens it automatically.

Burst mode

Scan receipt after receipt: each captured page becomes an invoice. In “One invoice” mode, pages form a single document.

File import

PDF, JPG, PNG or HEIC up to 10 MB, from Photos or the Files app.

Manual entry

No document? Create the invoice yourself in a few fields.

Offline

Without a network, scans wait on your iPhone and are sent as soon as you are back online.

Photos or camera

Take a photo, pick an existing image or scan: your choice.

Read

AI does the typing, you stay in control.

Pre-filled fields

Supplier, date, number, net amount, VAT, total and invoice lines.

VAT by rate

Each VAT rate is broken down, and Tickami checks that net + VAT = total.

To check

Missing or uncertain field, totals that don't add up: the invoice is flagged for you to review.

Automatic approval

Optionally, a well-read invoice goes straight to “Approved”.

Duplicates

An invoice you already saved is spotted; move the duplicate to the bin in one tap.

All documents

Invoices, receipts, restaurant or hotel bills, credit notes and quotes.

Organise

Every activity has its place.

Personal and work spaces

One space per activity or company, with its own colour. Work spaces track recoverable VAT.

One invoice, several spaces

Link an invoice to several spaces: it is only counted once in the overall total.

Categories

Accommodation, meals, fuel, telecoms, software… to see where the money goes.

Search

By supplier, invoice number, line label, note or exact amount.

Filters and sorting

By space, status or period; sort by date or amount.

Bin

A deleted invoice stays in the bin for 30 days, in case you change your mind.

Track

Your figures, without a spreadsheet.

Period summary

Total, number of invoices and invoices to check, for the month or period of your choice.

Breakdown by space

The weight of each activity in your spending, at a glance.

Recoverable VAT

Worked out automatically on your work spaces.

Latest scans

Your latest invoices on the home screen, with their status.

Analysis by space

For each space, your spending broken down by category and by supplier, over the period of your choice.

Every currency

Each space has its own currency; an invoice in another currency is converted at the rate of its date, and the home screen totals everything in your default currency.

Bank statements

All your transactions, finally readable.

Single or bulk import

One or several statements at once, as PDF, CSV or photo, from your iPhone or computer.

Read by AI

Each transaction is captured with its date, merchant, category, payment method and amount.

Table and filters

Period, type, categories, merchants, statements, spaces: multi-select filters, and several transactions selected at once.

Analytics

Cumulative cash flow, spending by category and merchant, money in and out by month, days of the week, comparison with the previous period.

Subscriptions spotted

Spending that comes back every month is detected automatically.

Reliable transactions

Transactions read cannot be edited; duplicates across statements are flagged.

Minimal data

IBAN, BIC, bank details and card numbers masked before anything is saved; the statement is deleted after it is read, only the transactions remain (unless you choose to keep it).

Export

Ready for your accountant.

CSV for Excel

All your invoice data, readable straight away in Excel.

ZIP of documents

The CSV with the photos and PDFs, named as in the table.

By space and period

This month, the quarter, the year or your whole history.

Share from your iPhone

Send the export by Mail, AirDrop or save it to Files.

Protect

Your data belongs to you.

Passwordless sign-in

Apple, Google, Microsoft or a 6-digit code sent by email.

Face ID

Lock the app; Tickami never receives your biometric data.

European hosting

Database and files in the European Union (Ireland), in private storage.

AI with your consent

Analysis only starts if you accept it; otherwise you enter the amounts yourself.

Account deletion

From the app, immediate and permanent: account, invoices and files.

French and English

The app follows your iPhone language, and can be changed in your profile.

Dark mode

Light, dark, or your iPhone setting: your choice in the profile.
