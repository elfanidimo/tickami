Subscription questions

Is the free trial risk-free?

Yes. The 7-day trial is offered on your first subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, in your Apple ID settings, and you will not be charged.

What happens with the 11th invoice in a space?

On the Free plan, a space holds up to 10 invoices; those in the bin don't count. Delete one, file the new one in your other space, or upgrade to Tickami+.

What if I stop Tickami+?

Your invoices stay viewable, editable and exportable: only additions beyond the Free plan limits are blocked. If you have more than 2 spaces, delete the extra ones (their invoices are kept) or resume Tickami+.

How do I pay and cancel?

The subscription is sold and charged by Apple, with your Apple ID. It renews automatically; manage or cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Refunds are requested from Apple.

Do prices include VAT?

Yes, prices shown are for France, VAT included. In other countries, the App Store shows the local price.

Can I share Tickami+ with my family?

No, Tickami+ is not available with Family Sharing: the subscription applies to your account.

I got a new iPhone: do I pay again?

No. Sign in to your Tickami account with the same Apple ID; if needed, use restore purchases in the app.

Does deleting my account cancel the subscription?

No. Remember to cancel Tickami+ in your Apple ID settings as well as deleting your account.
