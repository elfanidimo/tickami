In short

Tickami organises your invoices and analyses your bank statements; your data is used for that alone. No ads, no tracking, never sold. Hosted in the European Union. AI analysis only happens with your consent.

1. Data controller

Mohamed El Moukhtar EL FANIDI, publisher of Tickami, as an individual.

Address: 1 rue des Bleuets, 95100 Argenteuil, France

Phone: +33 6 95 48 88 04

Contact: contact@tickami.fr

2. Data processed

  • Account: email address, first and last name, Apple, Google or Microsoft account identifier if you use one.
  • Invoices: photos and PDFs, supplier, number, dates, amounts, VAT, items, category, notes.
  • Bank statements: bank, account name, last 4 characters of the IBAN, currency, period, balances, and for each transaction: dates, description, merchant, category, payment method, amount and balance. Never kept: the full IBAN, bank details and BIC, card numbers, the holder's name and address; bank numbers found in descriptions and file names are masked (last 4 digits at most) before anything is saved. The imported file (PDF, CSV or photo) is only kept if you chose so; otherwise it is deleted right after it is read.
  • Organisation: your spaces (name, type, colour) and which invoices belong to them.
  • Settings and privacy choices: language, auto-validation, accepted version of the terms, your choices (AI reading of invoices and statements, keeping files) and the history of these choices (date, value), which proves your consent.
  • Analysis log: date, AI model used and volume processed, to limit usage and track costs.
  • Subscription: chosen plan, trial, renewal and end dates. Tickami never receives your payment details: payment is handled by Apple.

3. Data that stays on your device

Recent searches, last spaces used, a copy of your profile and spaces (to open the app without a connection, erased when you sign out) and, when there is no connection, scanned invoices waiting to be sent. Face ID is handled by iOS: Tickami never receives your biometric data.

On the web version (app.tickami.fr), these items, your sign-in session and your appearance choice are kept in the browser's local storage, without any cookies; imported invoices are sent right away and are not kept in the browser.

4. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Providing the service (account, storage, organisation, search, totals, export): performance of the terms of use.
  • Managing your Tickami+ subscription and the Free plan limits: performance of the terms of use.
  • Analysing your invoices with AI: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Profile › Privacy. Without consent, nothing is sent to the AI and you enter the information yourself.
  • Analysing your bank statements with AI: a separate consent, withdrawn the same way. Without it, importing statements is turned off.
  • Sending sign-in emails: performance of the terms of use.
  • Preventing abuse (at most 60 analyses per hour): legitimate interest.

5. Who receives your data

  • Supabase: hosting of the account, database and files, in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Anthropic (Claude API): analysis of invoices and bank statements, only with your consent. Anthropic only receives the files to analyse, without your name or email, and does not use data received through its API to train its models.
  • Resend: sending sign-in emails, from servers located in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Apple, Google, Microsoft: sign-in with their account, only if you choose it.
  • Apple: sale and payment of the Tickami+ subscription.
  • RevenueCat: subscription tracking (purchases, renewals, end), under your Tickami identifier, without your name or email.

Anthropic, Resend and RevenueCat may process data in the United States. These transfers are covered by the safeguards provided by the GDPR (European Commission standard contractual clauses).

6. Retention

  • Account and invoices: as long as the account exists. File of an invoice read by AI: deleted once the invoice is validated if you chose not to keep files (an invoice entered by hand always keeps its photo).
  • Deleted invoices: 30 days in the trash, then permanently erased automatically.
  • Bank statements and their transactions: as long as the account exists, or until the statement is deleted in the app (transactions erased immediately). Statement file: deleted right after it is read unless you chose to keep it; if reading fails, kept for 24 hours at most so you can try again.
  • Large exports sent by email: archives kept for 7 days on our servers, then erased automatically.
  • Account deletion: immediate and permanent erasure of the account, invoices, bank statements and their transactions, files and analysis log.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct and erase your data, obtain a copy of it, object to processing or withdraw your consent.

Directly in the app: edit an invoice, export your invoices (Profile → Export), change your privacy choices and withdraw AI consent (Profile → Privacy), delete a statement and its transactions, delete your account. For any other request, such as erasing a bank statement, write to contact@tickami.fr: answer within one month. You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

8. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), bank numbers masked automatically in the database, statement files deleted after reading by default, kept files in private storage, data access restricted to its owner by database rules, temporary display links valid for 60 minutes, optional Face ID lock on iPhone.

9. Minimum age

Tickami is intended for people aged 15 and over.

10. The tickami.fr website

The website sets no cookies. To measure its traffic, each page view is recorded with its date, the visitor's IP address, the city and country derived from it (ipwho.is geolocation service) and the page viewed. This data, hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland), is only used for the publisher's traffic statistics, based on its legitimate interest; it is never sold or shared with third parties and is automatically erased after 90 days. You can object to it or ask to access or erase it at contact@tickami.fr. The app.tickami.fr web app does not use this traffic measurement.

11. Changes

If this policy changes significantly, you will be informed in the app before the change takes effect.
